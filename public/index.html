Made in our own factory in Kuala Lumpur

Since 2017 every bar has been cut, welded and coated under one roof in Desa Aman. See the production process

  1. Steel checked before it is cut

    Oval tubes with a 1.4 to 1.6 mm wall and 4.5 mm bracket plate are inspected batch by batch.

  2. Welded in a jig for each model

    Each bar is clamped in a steel jig made for one car, so it lines up with the factory bolt holes.

  3. Anti-roll bar ends forged at 950°C

    The ends of solid spring steel bars are heated in an induction coil and pressed into one-piece mounting blades.

  4. Coated in blue epoxy powder

    An electrostatic coat, oven-cured at 200°C, keeps off stone chips, oil and road salt.

Questions about fitting and warranty

Do the bars need drilling or welding?

No. Every bar bolts to factory mounting points with the brackets and bolts in the box.

Will the bars make the ride harsher?

The bars brace the body shell and leave the springs and dampers alone, so the car rolls less and rides the same.

How long is the warranty?

Lifetime, against structural failure, once the bar is registered with its serial or invoice number.

Which bars should I fit first?

Most drivers start with a front strut bar and a rear anti-roll bar, then add lower bars.
